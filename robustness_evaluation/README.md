# Robustness Evaluation

`01_attack_comparison` runs both attacks from `attacks/` against a real, non-cherry-picked sample of 15 sentences from the SST-2 validation split, and reports attack success rate, perturbation size (percentage of words changed, the text analogue of an L2 distortion), and query cost. HotFlip reaches 86.7% ASR while changing only 15.6% of words on average; Synonym Substitution reaches 53.3% ASR, changes more words (20.2%), and spends around 41 queries per sentence. The white-box attack is strictly better on every axis measured here, the same qualitative pattern seen between whitebox and blackbox attacks in the vision repo, though the gap is considerably wider in text than it is in images.

`02_budget_sweep` asks a question the comparison above cannot answer on its own: at what point does more perturbation budget stop helping. Sweeping HotFlip's flip count and Synonym Substitution's substitution count separately over a real sample of 10 sentences shows the two attacks behave very differently as budget grows. HotFlip is already at 90% ASR by a budget of 5 flips and barely improves by 8, meaning almost all of its power comes from the first handful of well-chosen changes. Synonym Substitution never plateaus in the range tested, climbing from 10% at a budget of 1 to 80% at a budget of 10, still visibly rising at the notebook's largest tested value. The white-box attack's gradient guidance finds the few changes that matter almost immediately; the black-box attack, searching blind, keeps needing more budget to catch up.
