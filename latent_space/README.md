# Latent Space

`01_embedding_trajectory` is the text analogue of the vision repo's PCA trajectory notebooks. Instead of a continuous epsilon, the attack strength here is discrete: the number of HotFlip flips applied so far. A PCA frame is fit on a background cloud of 150 real SST-2 sentence embeddings (DistilBERT's pre-classifier layer, 768 dimensions reduced to 2), which separates cleanly into a positive and a negative region along the first principal component (89.8% of variance). Three attack trajectories are then projected step by step, and each one starts in its original class's region and ends up crossing into the opposite one, direct evidence that HotFlip's word substitutions genuinely reorganize the model's internal representation of the sentence rather than exploiting a superficial output-layer quirk.
